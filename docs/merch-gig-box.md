# The KRDM gig box

Status: plan, written 2026-11-22. Nothing built yet. The physical companion to
[merch-app-brief.md](merch-app-brief.md): how merch is packed, carried, sold
from and counted at gigs, so that the app's locations, stands and closing
counts match what happens at the table.

## The problem

- Loading the merch, finding the right size, keeping the table tidy and
  counting afterwards are all slow and messy.
- The people watching the stand are often from another KRDM band, just keeping
  an eye on it until we're back. There's no time to explain a system to them.
- We don't know what sold at a gig.

What we're working with:

- Each band has 1–5 tee or hoodie designs, plus CDs, hats, lighters, picks and
  similar. A band's box can also hold label items (KRDM tees).
- Not much sells at one gig. Armaroth's last gig sold 1 CD, 3 tees and 1 zip
  hoodie; GMB packs more variety. So speed at the table matters much less than
  a tidy box, a stand anyone can watch, and knowing what sold.
- About 3 gigs a month across all the bands (36 published events in each of
  2024 and 2025, see [/statistika/](https://www.krdm.si/statistika/)).
- Two cars per gig. The merch should fit in **one box of reasonable size**.
- Stock lives at a band member's home. The Plc practice space is out, because
  the clothes take on its smell.
- Tables vary in size. Sometimes we can hang things, often not.
- Budget: **up to about €100**. People at Plc can build things.

## The idea: one standard box for every band

Every band gets **the same box with the same layout, colours and cards**. A
member of another KRDM band watching your stand already knows how your box
works, because it works like theirs, so nobody has to be briefed.

### The box: shirts filed like records

- **One 60×40 cm Euro stacking box with a lid.** It's a reasonable size for
  one car, stacks at home, and the lid keeps dust and smells out.
- **Shirts stand on their edge, like records in a crate.** Fold each shirt into
  a flat rectangle of the same size (use the folding board, below) and stand it
  on edge across the width of the box. Every shirt shows one edge from above,
  so you find a size and count by looking, without digging.
- **Dividers with tabs that stick up, as in a record bin:**
  - a **design divider** (black) at the start of each design, with a photo of
    the design and its price;
  - a **size divider** (white) for each size, with the size letter big on the
    tab and the tab wrapped in that size's colour (see the size colours below).

  The number of shirts between two tabs is the stock of that size. The
  dividers move, so the layout changes when the stock does.
- **Label items go at the back**, behind a design divider with the KRDM logo.
  That keeps the label's items, and in your head the label's money, apart from
  the band's.
- **One end of the box is for everything else.** CDs stand spine up in a row.
  The small-parts box (picks, lighters, badges) and hats sit next to or above
  them.
- **A size sticker on each shirt**, on the visible edge. A shirt taken out to
  show a customer then goes back to the right slot.

Starting layout, to adjust after the fold test:

```
 ←──────────────────────── 60 cm ─────────────────────────→
┌───────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────┐ ↑
│ CDs       │ ▌A  ▌S ║║║ ▌M ║║║║ ▌L ║║║ ▌XL ║║   ▌B  ▌M …  │
│ spine up  │                                              │ 40 cm
│───────────│ design A          design B        ▌KRDM …   │
│ small     │  ▌ = divider tab sticking up   ║ = one shirt │
│ parts,    │                                              │
│ hats      │                                              │ ↓
└───────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Do the fold test before buying the box.** Fold one tee and one hoodie of the
band's stock to the same rectangle and measure them. That decides:

- the box height: **22 cm** if the stock is small, **32 cm** if the shirts
  need to stand taller or the small items need to stack on top of the CDs;
- whether shirts stand in one row across the box or in two.

A folded tee is about 1.5–2 cm thick, a hoodie about 4 cm.

### Size colours

The same key everywhere: tabs, stickers, and later the app's size buttons. The
letter is always written too, so colour-blind sellers can read it. The colours
are ones you can buy as electrical tape.

| Size | XS | S | M | L | XL | XXL | 3XL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Colour | white | yellow | green | blue | red | black | grey |

Women's cuts get a **W** before the letter (WS, WM, …), with the same colour.

### The kit in the lid

Everything a guardian needs is in the box:

1. **A price list** to stand on the table: A4, in a clear sign holder or
   laminated and folded into a tent. It works on any table, including when
   nothing can be hung.
2. **The guardian card** (text below).
3. **The tally sheet** on a clipboard or in a sheet protector, with a pen.
   It's also the fallback when a phone is dead or the app isn't there yet.
4. **The contents list**: what the box holds when it's fully packed, and later
   a QR code to that box in the app.
5. **A cash pouch** with the change float.
6. **Pegs and some cord**, for when there's somewhere to hang one shirt of each
   design. Take the display shirt from the box, and sell it last.
7. Optional: a clip-on light, because venues are dark.

## Building it (a job for someone at Plc)

1. **Fold test**: fold the band's tees and hoodies to one rectangle, measure,
   then pick the box height.
2. **Folding board**: cut one from the leftover PVC board, the size of the
   folded rectangle, so every shirt folds the same. It lives in the box.
3. **Dividers**: cut them as wide as the box inside (measure it, roughly 36 cm
   for shirts across the width) and about 3 cm taller than the folded shirts.
   Cut a tab in the top edge. Stagger the tab positions (left, middle, right)
   so the tabs don't hide each other. Round the corners.
4. **Size tabs**: wrap each white tab in its size colour and write the letter
   big in permanent marker on both sides.
5. **Design tabs**: on each black divider, glue a printed photo of the design
   with its name and price, and cover it with clear tape. The label section
   gets the KRDM logo.
6. **End section**: a fixed divider about 15 cm from one end, glued or slotted,
   for the CDs, the small-parts box and hats.
7. **Lid kit**: print the price list, the guardian card, the tally sheet and
   the contents list (templates below). Laminate them, or put them in sheet
   protectors.

**Prototype with Armaroth first.** Try it at 2–3 gigs, fix what's annoying,
write down the final measurements here, and then build the other bands' boxes
the same way. Each band could pay for its own copy of the finished design.

## Using it

**Packing (at home, before the gig).** Fill the box up to the contents list,
and write down anything you couldn't fill. Put the float in the cash pouch and
a fresh tally sheet on top.

**At the stand.** Put out the price list, and hang a shirt of each design if
you can. The box stays open behind or under the table. Take shirts from the
front of their size slot and put them back in the same slot.

**When a guardian takes over.** Point at the card. That's the whole briefing.

**Closing.** Collect the tally sheet, then check the box against the contents
list: the difference is what sold. Compare it with the tally marks and the app
sales, and count the cash (float + sales). At home, fill the box up again or
leave it as it is for the next gig.

## Card texts

Sellers and guardians are Slovenian, so the cards are in Slovenian.

### Guardian card (lid, and next to the price list)

> **Paziš na našo stojnico? Hvala!**
>
> 1. Cene so na ceniku.
> 2. Denar gre v torbico.
> 3. Za vsak prodan kos naredi črtico na listu: vrstica = izdelek,
>    stolpec = velikost.
>
> Ne veš, kaj narediti? Reci kupcu, naj pride po koncertu. Prav nič narobe.

### Seller card (for band members, inside the lid)

> **Pred koncertom:** postavi cenik, obesi po eno majico vsakega motiva (če
> gre), škatla ostane za mizo. *(Ko bo aplikacija: skeniraj QR in odpri
> stojnico.)*
>
> **Med prodajo:** majico vzemi s sprednje strani njene velikosti, vrni jo
> na isto mesto. Prodajo vpiši v aplikacijo ali na list.
>
> **Po koncertu:** poberi list, preveri škatlo po seznamu vsebine (razlika =
> prodano), preštej denar (drobiž + prodaja).

### Contents list (lid)

```

```

Fill it from the band's stock. The numbers are the fill-up amounts, the same
every gig, so closing is just "what's missing".

## What the app takes from this

These are in [merch-app-brief.md](merch-app-brief.md):

- **A box template**: each gig box (a location) keeps its standard contents.
  "Pack" fills up to it, "Close" asks only what's missing, and the app prints
  the contents list with the box's QR code.
- **Guardian sales**: a band member enters a guardian's tally marks later, and
  the cash belongs to whoever takes the pouch.
- **Size colours**: the sale screen uses the size colours above.
