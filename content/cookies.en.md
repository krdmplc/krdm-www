The krdm.si website **does not use cookies** for analytics, tracking or advertising and does not collect personal data from visitors, so we don't ask for your consent.

## Visitor statistics

We count how many people visit the site and which pages they read with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and does not recognise you between visits. With each page view Cloudflare receives the page address, the page you came from, details of your browser and device, and your IP address, which it uses only to work out the country and does not store. We only see totals, never individual visitors.

## Embedded videos

Some pages embed videos from YouTube. A video only loads once you press play, in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play a video, YouTube may store data in your browser in line with [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Contact form

If you write to us using the [contact form](/en/contact/), your name, email address and message are stored in KRDM band (band.krdm.si), our app for merch and messages, which runs on Cloudflare (with its database in Western Europe). The app also forwards the message to us by email, through Cloudflare Email Service. To prevent abuse, we also store a one-way hash of your IP address, from which the address can't be read back. We only use all this to reply to you and never share it. Ask us if you'd like your message deleted.

## Cart and orders

The merch cart is kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage) until you send the order; it is not a cookie and is never sent to the server on its own. On the merch pages and in the cart, your browser asks the KRDM band app (band.krdm.si) what is still in stock; as with any visit, Cloudflare receives your IP address, and no cookies are involved.

When you send an order, your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), shipping address (if you choose delivery by post), note and the items you ordered are stored in the KRDM band app on Cloudflare (with its database in Western Europe), which also forwards the order to us by email, through Cloudflare Email Service. To prevent abuse, we also store a one-way hash of your IP address, from which the address can't be read back. We only use all this to handle your order and never share it. Ask us if you'd like your order data deleted.

## Colour theme

If you pick the light or dark theme in the page footer, your browser remembers the choice in local storage (localStorage). It is not a cookie, is never sent to the server, and you can clear it at any time by choosing "Automatic".

## Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure.

## Content editing

Editors who sign in to the content manager have the data needed for their login stored by the sign-in system. This does not apply to visitors.
