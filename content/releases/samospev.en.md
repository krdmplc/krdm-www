The Samospev EP is the fullest expression yet of Oti's maximalist popular music: four songs with a string trio, harpsichord and theremin. Self-released, it was launched on 14 November 2026 at Gala Hala in Ljubljana.

1. Orfej
2. Njen odblesk
3. Vampir
4. ILY variacije
