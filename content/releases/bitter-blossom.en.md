The debut album by Kranj's Mutism, which, as Urban Valjavec put it at the launch, they "started in the Precambrian". The album unfolds their signature sound, moving between melancholic, introspective atmospheres and more intense, rhythmically restless parts where the songs layer into wider soundscapes – from quiet, almost floating moments to dynamic peaks. It was launched on 24 April 2026 at Layerjeva hiša in Kranj.
