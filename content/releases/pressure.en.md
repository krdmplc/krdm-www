After five years of studio silence, Armaroth returned with the single and music video Pressure, the first in a series of singles heralding the follow-up to their debut Zenith.

"The track opens with a futuristic insert that lays the ground for an excellent, catchy industrial groove metal intro. […] As a heavy, excellent guitar solo turns the atmosphere more psychedelic, the vocals switch from clean singing to growls, and the ending is serene and unexpected." – [Paranoid](https://paranoid.si/recenzije/armaroth-pressure-single) (translated)
