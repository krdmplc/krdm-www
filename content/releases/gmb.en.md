After a ten-year break, Kranj death metallers GMB came back heavier, faster and greasier than ever. Their self-titled debut brings ten tracks of fast and, above all, groovy death metal, including the singles Nebelscheinwerfer (the very first song GMB wrote, back in 2012) and Which Animal Would You Like To Be?, both released in summer 2025. The album was launched on 19 June 2026 at Klub Jedro in Medvode.

"So enjoy this, the fury, anger and laughter, for: 'without music, life would be a mistake' (F.N.)"
