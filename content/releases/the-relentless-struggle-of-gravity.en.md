Douchean's second album, released on the UK label Singularity Recordings (SIN041), which introduced him as an experimental body music producer not afraid to explore the possibilities of EBM.

"The music of this album is extremely personal to me, every track is very closely related to experiences and feelings I have been going through and trying to grasp/come to terms with my entire life. The title refers to the constant and never ending inner conflict that I feel every living person goes through. The lifelong balancing act of ambition, ability, environment, desire and obligation. I suppose you could call it a kind of quest for harmony, which I think is reflected in the musical progression of the album."
