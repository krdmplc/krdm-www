The debut album by Kranj synthwave duo Neuromancer came out on the US label NewRetroWave (NRWR153): digitally and in limited runs on MiniDisc (50), cassette (50) and vinyl (150). It peaked at #1 on Bandcamp's synthwave and retrowave album sales charts.

"A high-powered darksynth album that will fuel your drive for synth. Spanning from classic synthwave sounds to retro-future fusions, Hardwired is a solid and well-rounded offering from Neuromancer." – NewRetroWave
