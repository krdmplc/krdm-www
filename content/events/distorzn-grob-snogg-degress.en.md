What goes best with the middle of winter? Cold black metal, of course! That's why January's Distoržn is painted black. We kick off with the young band Degress and their Postojna take on black/thrash, then Snøgg wander off into experimental waters, and the night peaks with a mass dedicated to chaos, performed by Grob.

### Snøgg

Experimental black, Velenje

Snøgg is an experimental black metal band from Velenje, founded in 2013. The original line-up consists of only two members, Ulv and Mørke, though guest performers join them most of the time, both on stage and on record. They call their style freeride black metal, because they don't limit themselves in exploring the vast sonic space of black metal and other extreme and alternative genres.
In six years the band has released a demo, three EPs and the debut album Chhinnamasta (June 2019), which was well received by critics and audiences alike and surpassed their earlier releases.
Snøgg are already working on new material for an upcoming record, expected in early 2020.

https://snogg.bandcamp.com/

### Degress

Black/thrash, Postojna

Degress have been playing for a good two years; the band was born in the graveyard of the bands Kripl and Sodndan. They play a nasty mix of black thrash metal and crust, and last year they released a demo, available from the band members. Expect the typical Postojna mayhem… uuuurgh

https://degress.bandcamp.com/
