## DISTORŽN

Bad guitarists' favourite effect is back: DISTORŽN! For everyone who's into screaming, running around in a chaotic circle and bad guitarists – here's something for you!

Moshead will premiere their new music video, Retribution, at Distoržn.

### Moshead

Thrash metal, Škofja Loka

Thrashers from Škofja Loka who have been tearing it up since 2011. They first made a name for themselves with the song Evil, then won the first season of the RTV show V svojem ritmu. In 2017 they released their first record, Game of Knowledge, and a second one is coming soon that will blow everything else away. Expect a dose of sheer mayhem – your neck will hurt the next day.

### Daimona

Atmospheric metal

The lyrics of the songs on our upcoming concept album are drawn from Dante's Inferno, from his legendary Divine Comedy. The path leads you through the circles of hell, where you will meet all the horrors of the sinners. We are Daimona, and we will take you down into the depths of the frozen hell.

Abandon all hope, ye who enter here!
