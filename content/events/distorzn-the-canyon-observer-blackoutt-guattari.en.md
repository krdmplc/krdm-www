So that December isn't killed off by cheerful capitalist music alone, we've prepared a stoner/sludge treat for you in the shape of another Distoržn! The dark soundscape will be built by the well-known The Canyon Observer, stoner heads Blackoutt from Postojna and the original Guattari from the coast.

The event is part of Potujoča železarna (Travelling Ironworks), a collaboration between Zavod O, the Škofja Loka youth institute and Pri Rdeči Ostrigi, KRDM and Železni Aktivizem.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

### The Canyon Observer

Post/sludge, Ljubljana

This five-piece provides the soundtrack to your worst nightmares, the glorious apocalypse that awaits us, or a traditional Sunday lunch with the family. It's all the same. Their experimental sound weaves delicate, atmospheric passages together with a furious, direct mix of black metal, doom and sludge. Backed live by impenetrable fog and blinding strobes, it makes for a heavy, menacing journey. A visceral journey that not even fluffy kittens can soften. They released their critically acclaimed debut "FVCK" in 2015 and toured all over Europe and even Cuba with it. The ever-curious crew has now come up with something new: on their second album "NØLL" they dig deeper into musical experimentation and spiral into the abyss of human souls and their surroundings. Step in: "It's there. It's nothing."

### Blackoutt

Stoner, Postojna

Blackoutt are a four-piece stoner rock band from Postojna who have spent years carving their way across club and festival stages at home and abroad with uncompromising riff-driven music wrapped in psychedelic guitars. They deliver a raw instrumental sound that doesn't miss vocal lines one bit. In 2013 the band released their debut, Behind, which shows a determined move towards storytelling and atmosphere, a direction that comes through even more clearly on their upcoming second album, The Culture Of.

### Guattari

Sludgy beatdown / groove, Slovenian coast

Guattari from the coast serve up an inflammable sludge/beatdown/groove cocktail that rarely leaves a listener indifferent. The band started in 2008 as a side project of members of Hiram and Shiv, then went quiet after a year. It was revived in spring 2019 with an expanded line-up of members of Shiv, Incarnated and Naio SSaion. In August 2019 the band released their first EP, "Demiurge", and played live for the first time.
