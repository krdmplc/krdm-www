KRDM presents DISTORŽN. This time tearing it up:

### Sovrag

Melodic death / black / thrash metal, Ljubljana

Sovrag is a metal band from Ljubljana, formed in 2018. In 2021 they released their first EP, Tribute to Death, followed in 2023 by their full-length debut Perfect Form from Chaos Born. Defining and pinning down SOVRAG is impossible, just as it is impossible to satisfy both humanity's lust for aggression and conflict and its longing for harmony, peace and joy. Musically, Sovrag is rooted in the old-school sounds of the 90s, somewhere between melodic death, black and thrash.

### Gorleben

Death / doom / black metal, Germany

Founded in 2010, GORLEBEN describe their style as a mix of death, doom and black metal, enriched with atmospheric and rudimentary elements. Their latest album, Menetekel, is a warning of the destruction of our civilisation, the countdown to which is already under way. Using time as its central element, GORLEBEN brings the predicted apocalypse into the present and makes it tangible from two temporal dimensions. Four songs inevitably trace the path of an uncertain future, marked not least by human greed. GORLEBEN divides; it looks from a bright future into a dark present.

### Damnation

Death metal, Laško / Zasavje

A crew from Laško and Zasavje making death metal that they like to darken with black metal influences and spice up with groove. They have released the single "Jack the Ripper" and are planning their first album.
