KRDM, together with TrainStation SubArt, presents a new series of metal nights:

## DISTORŽN

November will be brutal! We'll be visited by the deathcore wreckers Dethrone The Corrupted, MBurns are coming over from Croatia, and Odfuk are bringing Morbid Creation from Postojna (again).

### Dethrone The Corrupted

Deathcore, Slovenska Bistrica

Dethrone The Corrupted are five death metallers from Maribor and the surrounding area who joined forces and set off down a road of technical and massive death metal riffs. They introduced themselves to the public in 2014 with their first EP, followed by a second EP, "Sepiroth", in 2016. In 2018 they started work on their first album, "Sintrophia", whose first songs are already ringing out on stage.

<https://www.facebook.com/DethroneTheCorrupted/>\
<https://dethronethecorruptedofficial.bandcamp.com/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XDNAthZ7Lrk>

### MBurns

Modern metal, Crikvenica (HR)

MBurns are a metal band from Crikvenica, Croatia, founded in 2006. They already have plenty of shows at home and abroad behind them, at concerts, festivals and on tour. In 2011 they recorded their self-titled debut. It was followed by the covers EP “From Cover To Cover”, and last year they released their second album, “More to Life Than This”.

<https://www.facebook.com/mburnsofficial/>\
<https://mburnsofficial.bandcamp.com/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DRtOTaSpFS8>

### Morbid Creation

Brutal death metal, Postojna

Morbid Creation are the folk-pop death metal of Adelsberg. If you're a fan of rhythmically tight instruments, bassists who aren't just there for the image, professionally delivered shows and generally complex, melodic and ear-friendly riffs, then maybe this band isn't for you.

> »Total fetishisation of the rawness of a certain genre back when it was still in nappies, puking and shitting all over itself. At Distoržn.« – Mitja Kralj, 9 October 2016

<https://morbidcreation.bandcamp.com/>\
