KRDM presents DISTORŽN. This time tearing it up:

### GMB

Death metal, Kranj

Well known to the home crowd, these death metallers rank among the heavier bands in Slovenia. Their motto is "Cheerful, chubby, strong and bouncy", and it shows both in the music and in the members themselves. After many years of hibernation they are riding a mini revival this year: they have released two singles, an album is on the way, and plenty more is going on.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-v5NcV8_J0E>

### Ambra

Alternative / progressive metal, Ljubljana

A five-piece from the mists of the Ljubljana underground, combining the weight of alternative metal with a variety of progressive textures. Since starting out as an instrumental trio in 2015, the band has grown into a dynamic, emotionally charged whole, currently made up of Andrej Blatnik and Žiga Kroflič on guitars, Anže Vavpetič on bass, Robi Rep on drums and Jakob Rejec on vocals.

Ambra's sound comes from a wide spectrum of heavy music, drawing inspiration from bands such as Mastodon, Tool, Deftones, Clutch, Gojira, Nevermore and All Them Witches, while also weaving in dark atmospheres reminiscent of Chelsea Wolfe and Cult of Luna. The result is a sonic identity that is massive and riff-driven, yet also atmospheric and introspective.

### Big Bad Wolf

Speed / heavy metal

The Big Bad Wolf probably reminds you of the famous children's bedtime story. But a bedtime story they most certainly are not. Since 2011 they have been playing energetic speed/heavy metal – music without compromise, complication, needless frills, intricate melodies, digital effects or pre-recorded samples: just pure, raw, primal metal. Their unique mix of genres means there's something for everyone to bang their head to.
