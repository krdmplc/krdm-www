For the 10th anniversary of our rehearsal space (and everything else) in Planina, we present the best Plc Fest yet!

Plc Fest is the event where we present the local creators from the concrete lair we call Plc. We show what they've made over the past year and, above all, throw a first-class party. This year will be especially packed: first a short documentary about Plc shot by Filip Košnik, then a parade of excellent bands, and techno DJs Douchean and Ofnana_pesa will take us to the end of the night.


## Armaroth
death metal, plc

Armaroth is a five-headed entity dwelling somewhere in the obscure underground of Kranj. From its first recordings in 2008 to today, the band has established itself as one of the most brutal forces of the Slovenian metal scene. After the Burning Execution demo (2010), which only hinted at the feats to come, the band released the EP False Vision in 2013, held in high regard by fans and reviewers alike. After a few line-up changes, the singles Diamox and Deep Of Throats came out in 2016, announcing the long-awaited debut ZENITH. It was released in December of the same year and was very well received as something fresh and original on the metal scene. A second album is currently in the making, and in the meantime the guys keep gigging.

Although Armaroth prefers to create in its concrete lair, it shows its true face only when it takes the stage. With natural charisma and stage presence, the band poisons and seduces the audience. It is armed with an extremely loud and precise rhythm section, fat and twisted guitar riffs and psychedelic interludes, fronted by three hellish vocals before which fear itself bows.

## Neuromancer
synthwave, plc

Neuromancer is a synthwave duo from Kranj that has been livening up Slovenian music since 2018 by blending modern compositions with a nostalgic eighties feel. They crept onto the scene that year with their first single Hyperdrive and are currently working on their debut Inhuman, expected in the second half of this year. Live, expect everything from melancholic pianos and badass guitars to the heavy thunder of electronic sounds – the full range of emotions, caught between the present and the past.

## Didge
prog/psy rock, plc

Didge is a four-piece progressive rock band from Kranj, founded in 2012. Playing various alternative events around Slovenia, the band has won a small but devoted following in its home region.
In 2016 Didge released their debut Encounters in Endless Grey, and they are now focusing on their second album, announced with the single Vesper.

## Amalgam
dentist metal, plc

A Kranj band that stole its members from every local band and put together a peculiar musical amalgam. Their sound, rooted in progressive and mathematical styles of aggressive expression, gets flung down every possible avenue of schizophrenic melodies and brawling rhythms – true kranscendental music.

## Nature
death metal, plc

The band is known to almost every follower of fine metalwork in this country, and with some fresh blood in the line-up it's going to thunder thrillingly this time too. They rose from the ashes of Sinbolic and, despite a few breaks, keep reaping success and positive reviews. Their music is essentially progressive death metal, with nods to influences from other genres.

## Douchean
techno, plc

A young producer from Kranj who sonically explores abstract forms and unknown landscapes through dirty, distorted synths and pounding rhythms. His story takes him through every possible incarnation of dark underground dance music, and his sound is marked by strong emotion and a very large measure of experimentation.

## Ofnana_pesa
techno, plc

Ofnana_pesa is a DJ, producer, bassist, guitarist, event organiser… a lover of music. He started his musical journey in 2011 when he joined his first metal band, and has since been part of several bands and music projects. Because of his time in heavier bands, his techno sound is best described in three words: "hard, fast and evil".
