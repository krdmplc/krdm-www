Eolith Productions presents the first edition of CARNIUM DEATH FEST! Kranj will host American death metal innovators ARTIFICIAL BRAIN, Italian desecrators DEVOID OF THOUGHT and Croatian dissonant thrashers VAKTAL, as well as SIDEREAN, BLACK REAPER and NEFARIOUS VERMIN from Slovenia. The depths of Kranj have never roared so loud.

We put on Carnium Death Fest together with Eolith Productions, and the night was also part of the In Memoriam prof. Peter Hafner #13 festival.

### Artificial Brain

Progressive / dissonant death metal, USA

Drawing frequent comparisons to mind-altering progressive death metal acts, ARTIFICIAL BRAIN have distinguished themselves from their peers through an emphasis on rich, melancholic melodic figures, contextualizing the harmonic and emotive qualities of black metal within a more aggressive death metal framework. Dissonance is threaded through elegiac, otherworldly chordal structures; saturnine tremolo melodies float along in the high registers of the instruments; guitars and bass traverse distinct paths before locking into harmonic orbit. These elements, anchored by blast beats and bellowing guttural vocals, create a sense of contrast that has come to characterize the band's sound over the past decade.

### Devoid of Thought

Cosmic death metal, Italy

Making heads turn since 2016 with the cosmic death metal exposed in their demos, EPs and splits with Undeath and Into Coffin, Italy's Devoid of Thought finally release their 40-minute debut full-length "Outer World Graves", combining influences from bands like Timeghoul, Blood Incantation, Voivod and Incantation in a unique, bizarre and twisted vortex.

"A sick, twisted piece of cosmic death metal that is always brutal and interesting but always pushing the listener to the edge of nothingness." (Metal Temple)

"Spin yourself into the sonic vortices they create, it's indeed easy to become devoid of thought, wholly consumed by the experience." (No Clean Singing)

### Vaktal

Sci-fi tech-thrash, Zagreb

Vaktal is a four-piece sci-fi tech-thrash band from Zagreb, Croatia. The band formed in the quarantine era of 2020 and released its first demo EP, Vitriolic Revolt, in the winter of 2021.

### Siderean

Astral death metal, Ljubljana

Astral voyagers Siderean venture into grotesque celestial sonic realms, melding deep space atmosphere and dissonant death metal abstraction with grand mythopoeic narratives. Their roots grow out of far-reaching influences from the old school and unfurl toward the stars. The band's debut, Lost on Void's Horizon, released by Norway's Edged Circle Productions, declares their first complete vision.

<https://www.facebook.com/siderean>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wQgWG6v7B2c>

### Black Reaper

Death metal, Slovenia

Black Reaper is a five-piece death metal band from Slovenia. The band started out playing thrash metal, releasing their first album, "Blood Moon Rising", in 2017. A year later we released our second album, "Birth Of Extinction", which already showed death metal elements. The second album was followed by a line-up change, with which the band moved into death metal waters, and that is how we released our third album, "Under The Dying Sun".

<https://www.facebook.com/Black-Reaper-432968253452333>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J1DWQrYUOUM>

### Nefarious Vermin

D-beat death metal, Postojna

D-beat infested death metal duo with occasional black metal rites from the depths of Kontejnr. Postojna's d-beat infested death metal duo (featuring members of ex-Morbid Creation, FTD, ex-Zabojnik, CarlxJohnson, ex-Sedmina) surprises with a slab of uncompromising, slimy, rat-infested death metal with occasional black metal rites that make you feel like you are drowning in pork fat. Outbursts of bestial d-beat make sure you don't miss that they grew up listening to noisy hardcore punk such as Disclose. Since seeing Spectral Voice live they have become obsessed with glooming atmospheres and unrelenting eerie riffs, onto which they added their own filthy influences – and thus the cult that is Nefarious Vermin came to life!
