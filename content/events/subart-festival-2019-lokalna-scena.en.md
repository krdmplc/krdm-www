The local scene, open air, as part of the 11th Subart festival: concerts at the B&B poligon first, then we move into Train with the DJs. A party till dawn guaranteed!

## Concerts @ B&B poligon

**Contribution: 8 €**

### Raggalution

Reggae / dub, Kranj

Since 2011 Kranj's Raggalution have been stirring their reggae stew, spiced with dub, funk and a pinch of psychedelia. Touring regularly, they took their 2013 studio debut Mask of Liberty from Greece to the Netherlands. In April 2017 Raggalution released their second album, Gatekeeper, which they are now presenting on stages across Europe. Both albums are a free download from their website.

<https://www.facebook.com/Raggalution/>\
<https://raggalution.com/>

### Nature

Progressive death metal, Kranj

The band is known to almost every follower of noble musical metalwork, and with some fresh blood in the ranks it will thunder thrillingly this time too. They rose from the ashes of Sinbolic, and despite a few breaks along the way they keep scoring successes and earning positive reviews. Their music is progressive death metal at its core, with a flirtation with influences from other genres.

### Didge

Progressive rock, Kranj

Didge are a four-piece progressive rock band from Kranj, founded in 2012. By playing various alternative events around Slovenia, the band has won a small but devoted following in its home region.

In 2016 Didge released their debut, Encounters in Endless Grey, and they are now focusing on their second album, announced with the single Vesper.

<https://www.facebook.com/didgeplc/>

### Smetnaki

Ska / punk / funk, Kranj and Tržič

Smetnaki ("The Rubbish Lot") are a young band from Kranj and around Tržič, formed under the influence of ska, punk and funk. Jokingly, they say of themselves: »Just as in everyday life you come across a lot of rubbish, at a Smetnaki concert you hear rubbish too, because the crappiest music you could possibly hear comes out of our instruments. If you throw a stone down a hill, it will probably make a better sound than what comes out of our instruments.«

### March Of War

Blackened melodic death metal, Medvode

March Of War are a blackened melodic death metal band from Medvode. It was founded in 2017 at the wish of its lead guitarist. The band is currently on its way to its first album of original material, due out in 2020.

<https://www.facebook.com/marchofwarofficial/>

### Neuromancer

Synthwave, Kranj

Neuromancer are a synthwave duo from Kranj who have been spicing up Slovenian music since 2018 by weaving together modern compositions and a nostalgic eighties feel. That was when they sneaked onto the scene with their first single, Hyperdrive; they are currently working on their debut, Inhuman, which we can expect in the second half of this year.

<https://www.facebook.com/neuromancersynth/>

### Grimase

Blues rock, Tržič

Newcomers from Tržič playing blues rock.

### Ofnana_pesa

Techno, Kranj

Ofnana_pesa is a DJ, producer, bassist, guitarist, event organiser … A music lover. He started his musical journey in 2011 when he joined his first metal band, and has since taken part in a number of bands and musical projects. As a result of playing in heavier bands, his techno sound is best described in three words: "hard, fast and evil".

<https://www.facebook.com/ofnana.pesa/>

### Douchean

Techno, Kranj

A young producer from Kranj who sonically explores abstract forms and unknown landscapes through dirty, distorted synthesisers and pounding rhythms. His story takes him through every possible incarnation of dark underground dance music, and his sound is marked by strong emotion and a very large measure of experimentation.

<https://www.facebook.com/douchean/>
