## Ostrigina metalurgija

28 September 2019 at 21.00

The event is part of Potujoča železarna ("the Travelling Ironworks"), a collaboration between Zavod O (the Škofja Loka youth institute), Pri Rdeči Ostrigi, KRDM and Železni aktivizem.

### Armaroth

Psychedelic death metal, Kranj

Armaroth is a five-headed entity dwelling somewhere in the obscure underground of Kranj. From their first recordings in 2008 to the present day, the band has established itself as one of the most brutal forces of the Slovenian metal scene. After the demo Burning Execution (2010), which only hinted at the real feats to come, the band released the EP False Vision in 2013, highly regarded by fans and reviewers alike. After a few line-up changes, 2016 first brought the singles Diamox and Deep Of Throats, heralding the eagerly awaited debut ZENITH. It came out in December of the same year and was very well received as something fresh and original on the metal scene.

### Nature

Death metal, Kranj

Nature are the least serious band ever. Impossible to work with. They don't send a description. They don't have their own cables. Otherwise, interesting death metal. A bit jazzy, a bit wtf.

### Decage

Death / groove metal, Velenje

Decage are a band from Štajerska that grew out of the remains of the local bands Chimera and Heretic. After several line-up changes they finally settled on Sebastijan Založnik on vocals, Klemen Ribič on bass, Izi Felicijan on rhythm guitar and Aco Trivunovič on drums. A young band with few miles on stage but plenty of experience from their previous bands. They can't quite define their music, but they say it's some kind of modernist/groove metal.

<https://www.facebook.com/decageband/>
