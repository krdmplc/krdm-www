The very first Plc Fest. The bunker's landlord had sent us a very high electricity bill, and being broke kids we had to do something. Music was what we had in common, so we decided to put on a concert and raise some money to keep Plc going.

Entry was free, with voluntary donations. Armaroth celebrated their 5th anniversary, and Nature played their last show before a long break – without their bassist, with a third guitarist and after a single rehearsal.

### Plains of Despair

Technical death metal, Plc

The youngest band from Plc.

### Nature

### Armaroth

Death metal, Plc – 5th anniversary

New merch on top of the usual, and an afterparty with music picked by the band.
