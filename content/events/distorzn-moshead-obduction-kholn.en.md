After a successful first season of Distoržn, the second one is here – and it's even better! In October, as part of the Potujoča železarna (Travelling Ironworks) project, we're bringing together three towns: Kranj, Škofja Loka and Velenje. A brutal thrash wind will blow in "from Loka" in the form of Moshead, Velenje will bring the smell of coal with trve kvlt black metallers Kholn, and after a long, long time Obduction, the oldest still active band from Kranj, return to their home stage.

The event is part of Potujoča železarna, a collaboration between Zavod O, the Škofja Loka youth institute and Pri Rdeči Ostrigi, KRDM and Železni Aktivizem.

## Moshead

Thrash, Škofja Loka

A thrash metal band from Škofja Loka that started out in 2011. Soon after forming they began writing their own songs; the first one they recorded was Evil, and many more followed. In 2013 they played Vičstok alongside Tabu, Vlado Kreslin and others, and the same year they won the emerging bands contest in Kranj, which earned them a slot at the closing concert of Teden mladih with S.A.R.S. and Elvis Jackson. In 2016 they won the first season of the RTV show V svojem ritmu, and in 2017 they released their first album, Game of Knowledge. They are no strangers to big stages either, having shared them with most of the giants of Slovenian metal as well as foreign acts such as Vicious Rumors and Nervosa. Their second album, already in the works, is due in 2020.

## Obduction

Metal, Kranj

Obduction have been active since 1994, which puts them among the first shoots of the Kranj metal scene. They are the only band of their generation still going, probably the oldest active metal band in Kranj and the wider area, and beyond their own genre too. Despite the years, their music sounds fresh and contemporary.

https://obduction.bandcamp.com/
https://www.facebook.com/obductionband/

## Kholn

Black, Velenje

Kholn grew out of a one-man band in 2013, when Acot was joined by Izi on guitar, Korelc on vocals and Morgoth on drums. Original material started taking shape right away, and after half a year of rehearsing they played their first show in Velenje. Shows in Murska Sobota, Tolmin and last year's Kunigunda festival followed. That's also when they released the demo Wrath of the Forgotten, which heralds their first album, due in the near future.

They have already shared the stage with Greece's Rotting Christ and Norway's Abbath, among others. Kholn draw their black metal inspiration from bands such as Satyricon, Gorgoroth, Mayhem and Darkthrone.
