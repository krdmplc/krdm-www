KRDM presents the irregular traditional festival of the Kranj underground: PLC FEST 2026! Plc Fest is a festival of the musicians who rehearse in an air-raid shelter beneath Kranj.

This year's line-up is especially varied – we have no fewer than 8 acts, playing metal, hardcore, prog rock, punk and everything in between! They range from bands nobody has heard of yet, through old-timers making a comeback, to regulars of Kranj's stages. Come for plenty of good music and good company – and there might be a surprise or two as well!

As always, we're hosted by Trainstation Subart. We start at 19:00.
