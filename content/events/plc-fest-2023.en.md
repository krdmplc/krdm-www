KRDM presents the next iteration of the Kranj underground's irregular traditional festival: PLC FEST 2023! Plc Fest is a festival of the artists who rehearse in a certain shelter deep under Kranj.

This year the hardest workers were all hell-raisers, so this Plc Fest is a total metal rampage. Come for plenty of good music and good company – and there might be a surprise or two as well!

This year's Plc Fest also marked the 10th anniversary of the very first Plc Fest.
