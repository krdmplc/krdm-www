KRDM, together with TrainStation SubArt, presents

## DISTORŽN

Necks will snap on the backs of heavy riffs, groovy rhythms will drive the mosh pit, and Pelinkovec will soothe throats gone dry from screaming. In short, metal chaos will reign when Kranj welcomes the well-known Sober Assault, the well-known-in-Romania Saddayah and the slightly less known but no less good Decage!

**Running order:** 21.00 doors · 21.30 Decage · 22.30 Saddayah · 23.30 Sober Assault

### Sober Assault

Thrash / death metal, Grosuplje

Sober Assault are a four-piece from Grosuplje, formed in 2013. They are an unstoppable musical assault on the listener, built from a symbiosis of thrash and melodic death metal with progressive elements and raw aggression and violence. The result is a combination of fast, complex and aggressive guitar riffs, raw vocals, thundering bass lines, fast and violent drums and unexpected harmonised guitar melodies.

### Saddayah

Death metal, Romania

Hyperblasts, heavy riffs, catchy melodies and tricks put Saddayah on the metal map as a one-of-a-kind band. Founded in 2014, it has since grown into an excellent band playing death metal with blackened and technical touches. Last year they released their debut, "Apopheny of Life", followed by an extensive tour. They will stop by as part of their first European tour.

<https://www.facebook.com/SaddayahBand/>\
<https://saddayahband.bandcamp.com/releases>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ikCRoQn1Tdc>

### Decage

Death / groove metal, Velenje

Decage are a band from Štajerska that grew out of the remains of the bands Chimera and Heretic. After several line-up changes they finally settled on the following: Sebastijan Založnik on vocals, Klemen Ribič on bass, Izi Felicijan on guitar and Aco Trivunovič on drums. A young band with few miles on stage but plenty of experience from their previous bands. They can't quite define their music, but they say it's some kind of modernist/groove metal.

<https://www.facebook.com/decageband/>\
