KRDM presents the next Distoržn:

Moshead and Black Camo present their albums, with special guests Ghastly Ashes

### Moshead

Thrash metal, Škofja Loka

A thrash crew from Škofja Loka that started out in 2011. Soon after forming they began writing their own songs, and the first one they recorded was called Evil. They won the first season of the RTV show V svojem ritmu. In 2017 they released their first record, Game of Knowledge, and this year they served up the album We Were Led Astray, which they will present at this very event.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yss9wGrLRoE>

### Black Camo

Thrash metal

Black Camo are a four-piece thrash metal band offering heavy, groovy breakdowns on top of fast, energetic riffs. Formed around 2021, they have already played plenty of Slovenian stages and festivals, and you can count on them to get the pit going. Not long ago they released their debut The Cult of Black Camo, which you can of course hear on every streaming platform, and they will present it on our stage on 10 May. They promise you an unforgettable night.

<https://open.spotify.com/album/09SsZTC9mwxK3YF6ACRvGM>\
<https://youtu.be/7oX0tpOXxE0>

### Ghastly Ashes

Groove metal, Primorska

A six-piece groove metal band from the Primorska region, combining melody with brutal guitar shredding and fast beats, and emotional lyrics with two vocalists, each "disgusting" in their own way.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JmrZyF7TXI>
