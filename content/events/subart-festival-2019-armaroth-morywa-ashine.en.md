A metal night at the 11th Subart festival, put on by TrainStation SubArt together with KRDM: hometown heroes Armaroth, Morywa from Štajerska and Ashine from Ptuj.

### Armaroth

Psychedelic death metal, Kranj

Armaroth is a five-headed entity dwelling somewhere in the obscure underground of Kranj. From their first recordings in 2008 to the present day, the band has established itself as one of the most brutal forces of the Slovenian metal scene. After the demo Burning Execution (2010), which only hinted at the real feats to come, the band released the EP False Vision in 2013, highly regarded by fans and reviewers alike. After a few line-up changes, 2016 first brought the singles Diamox and Deep Of Throats, heralding the eagerly awaited debut ZENITH. It came out in December of the same year and was very well received as something fresh and original on the metal scene.

Although Armaroth prefer to create in their concrete den, they only show their true face once they take the stage. With natural charisma and stage presence, the band poisons and seduces the audience. They are armed with an extremely loud and precise rhythm section, fat and twisted guitar riffs and psychedelic touches, while at the front roar three infernal voices before which fear itself bows down.

### Morywa

Nu death metal, Štajerska

Morywa is a five-headed beast from Štajerska whose name means "noise", and it lives up to that name with shamelessly low-tuned guitars and a furious rhythm section with which it grinds through the land, opening the ear canals of any life that crosses its path. The first sightings date back to 2013, when the organism began wrecking the stages where it feels most at home, and it was officially put on the map in 2016 with the release of its debut Vamana Ira, which was very well received on the Slovenian scene.

After a few member changes, the band is once again ominously active in a new line-up and is preparing a new record, due out in the second half of the year of our Lord 2019.

<https://www.facebook.com/morywa/>

### Ashine

Thrash / death metal, Ptuj

Ashine are a metal band from Ptuj, active since 2013. After a few line-up changes, in 2015 they released their debut EP, "Ashine", which served as a blueprint for their further work. The band draws its influences mostly from old-school thrash and death metal, but isn't afraid to experiment with vocals and instrumental melody. They have announced an album called "Force of Nature" for 2019.

<https://www.facebook.com/ashineband/>
