KRDM, together with TrainStation SubArt, presents

## DISTORŽN

Before you get wedged in an abundance of Christmas binges and drown in your own excretions on New Year's Eve, a gift package awaits you with your favourites – the penises! Yes, Penitenziagite are coming, the “elders” of the Kranj underground. They'll be joined by the energetic thrashers Hangar 55\* (the asterisk is silent) and by a bunch of thrashers who want to play grindcore – Glista!

**Running order:** 21.00 Glista · 22.00 Hangar 55\* · 23.00 Penitenziagite

### Penitenziagite

Death metal, Kranj

Penitenziagite won over Slovenian audiences at their very first concert with their mix of black and death metal, and later grew into a mature death metal band that plays around with thrash guitar solos. They have released two demos and played every well-known Slovenian stage, the main stage of Metalcamp and the small stage of Metaldays. They leave no listener cold. In 2015 they released their first album, Humanity Galore. The band is currently working hard on its next album.

<https://www.facebook.com/penitenziagite.band/>\
<https://penitenziagite.bandcamp.com/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FgvHffTi6dU>

### Hangar 55\*

Thrash metal, Medvode

Hangar are a five-piece from Mengeš/Medvode, founded in 2014. The band is made up of five members with plenty of experience from playing in earlier bands. They mostly play heavy metal/hard rock with the occasional touch of punk. In 2016 they released their debut, First Aid, which they have been presenting on stages around Slovenia. Meanwhile they have gathered enough material for a new record, which they plan to release next year.

<https://www.facebook.com/hangar55/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npuBDOqsS9w>

### Glista

Grindcore, Ljubljana

Glista ("Worm") peeked out of its egg in December 2015 and recorded its first demo in autumn 2017. In March 2018 they shut themselves in the studio and recorded their debut, Death Machinery, out on 23 November 2018.

A musical superposition of punk and metal, reflecting a broad range of influences from old-school hardcore punk, crust, death, thrash and black metal bands, and last but not least some classical music and a pinch of disco, has put Glista on stage alongside representatives of many genres, among them Ratos de Porão, Nulla Osta, Bolesno Grinje, Extreme Smoke 57, Brujeria and Gutalax.

<https://www.facebook.com/GlistaGrindcore/>\
<https://glista.bandcamp.com/releases>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V11rjHWXL6k>
