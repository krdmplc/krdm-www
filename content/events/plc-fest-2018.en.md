\+ welcome drink +\
\+ free Denktimes Compilation Vol. III +

PLC is the name of a shelter in Kranj that is home to seven bands, an informal school of programming and many other creative activities. The collective, some 30 variously hairy heads strong, is constantly working to improve its workspaces and raise the quality of the alternative scene in Gorenjska.

But creating and maintaining workspaces and gear doesn't come cheap, and the winter chill and the resulting need for heating only squeeze already thin wallets even harder, so the lads decided to put on a fundraiser concert, PLC FEST! The event will present three of Plc's musical projects, and the ticket money will go towards the winter heating bill and towards improving the rehearsal and work spaces.

### Didge

Progressive / psychedelic rock, Kranj

A progressive/psychedelic rock bunch of old friends from around Kranj. From humble beginnings in metal, over the years the band has developed a distinctive blend of all kinds of musical styles, unorthodox and dynamic song structures and conceptual lyrics which, for all their layers, keep a common thread and stay fairly simple and universal. Since releasing their debut Encounters in Endless Grey in October 2016, the band has thrown itself fully into new ideas, which you'll be able to hear at upcoming shows and on upcoming releases.

### Mutism

Psychedelic / progressive, Kranj

Four cowboys sit around a storage heater eating chocolate pudding. Who'll be left without a spoon after dinner? More than enough meat for everyone. Rubbish lies everywhere. Cacti dance in the distance. Are you drinking enough water?

### Inhibis

Post-metal, Murska Sobota

Music so massive that through its sound you will give birth to yourself. But Inhibis are not just massive waves of noisy riffs on top of a destructive rhythm section: they also have calmer, atmospheric parts, and after a brutal beating they politely invite us on a romantic date. Even if both happen only in sonic form.

<https://youtu.be/U7WUgOpna-8>

### Nesit

Plc, Kranj

A bunch of young music enthusiasts trying to combine all kinds of musical styles into interesting combinations and fairly listenable music.
