KRDM in collaboration with TrainStation SubArt presents:

## DISTORŽN

After far too long a forced break, the loudest chain of concerts returns to Kranj's stages! This time the local youngsters Inferior Minds will serve you a dose of modern death metal, Guattari will shock you with their inventive approach, and Armaroth will bring the night to its peak.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

### Armaroth

Psychedelic death metal, Kranj

Armaroth come from the Kranj underground and over the last decade have established themselves as one of the more prominent forces of the Slovenian metal scene. In 2013 they released the EP False Vision, by now a true death metal classic. Their full-length debut Zenith followed in 2016, on which the band upgraded their sound with more modern touches and pointed to a style all of their own. Live, their natural charisma and stage presence win over audiences at home and across Europe.

### Guattari

Sludgy beatdown / groove, Slovenian coast

Guattari from the coast serve up an inflammable sludge/beatdown/groove cocktail that rarely leaves a listener indifferent. The band started in 2008 as a side project of members of Hiram and Shiv, then went quiet after a year. It was revived in spring 2019 with an expanded line-up of members of Shiv, Incarnated and Naio SSaion. In August 2019 the band released their first EP, "Demiurge", and played live for the first time.
