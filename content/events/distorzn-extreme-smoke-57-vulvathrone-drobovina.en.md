## DISTORŽN

Grind, porn, internal organs … these are the themes of February's Distoržn. Drobovina will open with a self-proclaimed letdown, Vulvathrone will carry on with a porn party, and the legendary Extreme Smoke 57 will deliver a slithering grand finale.

**Running order:** 21.00 doors · 21.30 Drobovina · 22.30 Vulvathrone · 23.30 Extreme Smoke 57

### Extreme Smoke 57

Grind / noisecore

Extreme Smoke 57 formed in 1990 as the first grind/noisecore band in Slovenia, split up in 1993 and rose from the dead in 2008. The band has played countless shows all over Europe and has quite a rich discography, to which they added a new studio release, »Corruption Deteriorates«, at the start of this year. Grind or be grinded!

### Vulvathrone

Brutal death / porngrind

Depraved schemes and designs to found a band inspired by every kind of perversion were the main topic of many an evening mercilessly saturated with alcohol fumes, during which Vulvathrone, with drunken vehemence, gleefully announced to the drowsy heavy-metal community the imminent arrival of a cult porngrind rampage the likes of which Slovenia had never seen. In their den, cacophonous gems, atonal tunes and unbridled orgies were spawned one after another, and so the hope-filled group of musicians set out on an immeasurably tiring and soul-destroying journey. As a four-headed monster we have played many concerts and festivals with bands such as Dying Fetus, Napalm Death, Nile, Incantation, Abrasive, Sanatorium, Lividity and more.

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzkSm-JI-KY>\
<https://vulvathrone.bandcamp.com/>

### Drobovina

Crust / grind / hardcore

Silence. The boundless darkness of space. The Big Bang. The birth of something primordial. A synonym for light. All of this is … Drobovina ("Guts"). A band whose members grew up locked in their grandma's basement in Šenčur with only one musical backdrop to their childhood: the sound of disappointment. They try to recreate it with their elemental, at times folk-flavoured music, which combines din, speed and recklessness surprisingly deftly. There will be noise.
