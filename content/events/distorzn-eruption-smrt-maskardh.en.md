### Eruption

Thrash metal, Ljubljana

Formed in late 2004, Eruption is a thrash metal band from Ljubljana, Slovenia. Throughout the years, the band has always kept fast, guitar-based and infectiously melodic metal as their mission statement, culminating in the releases of Lifeless Paradise in 2009, Tenses Collide in 2012 and Cloaks of Oblivion in 2017. All three full-lengths, accompanied by 2014's Live Transmissions EP, saw the band explore classic machine-gun thrash riffing accompanied by the soaring melodic vocals and sci-fi musings of vocalist Klemen Kalin.

The energetic blend of influences allowed the band to present its music in a dynamic, furious stage show that saw Eruption share the stage with names such as Warbringer, Nuclear Assault, D.R.I. and Iced Earth, perform on the main stage of festivals such as MetalDays and Underwall, and garner excellent live responses from around Europe.

Tellurian Rupture, the band's fourth full-length effort, released on 19 August 2022 through From the Vaults, sees Eruption's sound expanding even further. Where Cloaks of Oblivion introduced strong US power metal influences, Tellurian Rupture takes a more technical approach with an overall darker, heavier atmosphere.

### Maskardh

Progressive death metal, Ljubljana

Maskardh is a band that brings a progressive metal mauling with plenty of balls and juice to the stage. The Ljubljana-based five-piece wants to widen the pupils and drop the jaws of everyone present at sonic massacres all over the country. Soon the storm of raw energy will resonate through your membranes too.

### Smrt

Spiritual death metal

SMRT is a "spiritual death metal" band that writes about the degradation of the mind and everything that marks us as human beings. Their music is an insight into them as individuals and so will always grow with them. They have 2 EPs, 1 live album and 1 LP under their name, and are currently finishing their next full-length, "What We Call Home".
