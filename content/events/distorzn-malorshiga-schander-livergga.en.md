KRDM presents a Black Metal DISTORŽN!

### Malorshiga

Black metal, Slovenian Istria

This black metal band embodies Slovenian Istria in sound, word and image, singing of the nature, culture, history and mythology of the land it comes from. Malorshiga are currently preparing to release their second LP, Vento, an ode to the Adriatic winds. In the run of concerts before the release they are playing songs from their debut Kvlt ov Vitis et Olea alongside the new material.

<https://malorshiga.bandcamp.com/>

### Schänder

Black metal, Klagenfurt (Austria)

SCHÄNDER from Klagenfurt, Austria, were founded in 2019, when Gerry Greyskull, Lex Savage, Kleschkolt Kole and Infernal Zermallner united under the banner of old-school black metal to unleash death and decay upon the world.

<https://schaender.bandcamp.com/>\
Interview: <https://paranoid.si/intervjuji/schaender-intervju-2026>

### Livergga

Black metal, Koper

Livergga are a black metal band from Koper, Slovenia, focused on the nostalgic, raw and "kvlt" sound of old-school black metal.

<https://livergga.bandcamp.com/>
