RELEASE THE DEAD!

A death metal treat celebrating the release of the album "GMB" is coming to Medvode! Bring your earplugs and wash your hair, because Jedro is going to be bursting at the seams!

### GMB

Death metal, Kranj (album release)

After a ten-year break, Kranj death metallers GMB have returned to the home scene – heavier, faster and greasier than ever. At Klub Jedro they will present their new self-titled debut, "GMB". The album has 10 songs and will be played in full on the night. The songs carry the influence of metal legends combined with the mustiness of the Kranj bunker. Expect fast and, above all, groovy death metal that hypnotises listeners' necks into uncontrollable movement (like a cobra to the sound of Indian flutes). We hope the stage holds up.

<https://linktr.ee/gmb.krdm>

### Razvalina

Old-school death metal

War atrocities, genocide, addiction, the threat of digital intelligence, the little guy's struggle against the capitalist superpowers, the agony and exhaustion of the modern world – all of it delivered through the lyrics, heavy greasy riffs and fast blast beats of the old-school death metal trio Razvalina. You definitely won't be disappointed – it'll be hitting from every side. UGH!

<https://www.facebook.com/Razvalina.official>

### Morywa

Nu death metal, Štajerska

From the land of Štajerska comes the five-piece nu death metal band Morywa. The band is a beast that has been wrecking Slovenian and foreign club and festival stages since 2014. Their latest release is the album "II" (2025), which defined the Morywa sound even more deeply – crushing vocals, scorching guitars and mighty rhythms! A show you mustn't miss.

<https://www.facebook.com/morywa>
