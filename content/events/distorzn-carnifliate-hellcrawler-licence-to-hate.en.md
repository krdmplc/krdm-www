KRDM, together with TrainStation SubArt, presents

## DISTORŽN

After two quiet months, Distoržn is back! This time an honest death assault awaits you, no beating about the bush. Licence To Hate will play hymns from the cemeteries of Postojna, Hellcrawler will bring a true death assault, and whatever is left of your bones will be broken by old acquaintances Carnifliate.

**Running order:** 21.30 Licence To Hate · 22.30 Hellcrawler · 23.30 Carnifliate

### Carnifliate

Death / grind, Velenje

The band formed in 2013, took its final shape at the start of 2014, and played its first concert in Velenje that same year. At first they played covers of bands like Obituary, Decapitated and Lamb of God, but soon started writing their own music. The first songs were Slaughterlust, Blood On My Tombstone and God’s Cock. A long string of concerts all over Slovenia and in neighbouring countries followed. They also played festivals such as Live After Death, Vratolom, Breka Fest and Kunigunda, and shared the stage with big names like Benighted, Gutalax, Acranius, Analepsy and Noctiferia. Their debut Slaughterlust came out in March 2016, and a year later the band went on its first tour (the Slaughterlust tour) through Hungary, Romania and Bulgaria to promote the album. In March 2018 came a second tour (the March of Death tour) across the whole of Romania and also Croatia, with Snøgg.

The band is currently working on material for its second album, Disgusting Festivities, due out in 2019; the single Compel Insemination already came out last year as a taste of the album.

### Hellcrawler

Death 'n' roll, Nova Gorica

Hellcrawler are a gang of decaying, cynical earthlings living among the ruins of wrecked Western Slovenia. They found an indecent but universal solution in a symbiosis of punk violence, stoner atmosphere and heavy riffs. Their soundtrack to the downfall of humanity is a motorised assault of old-school Swedish death metal and d-beat that reveals their main source of inspiration: the decaying aesthetics of post-apocalyptic and grindhouse cinema.

<https://www.facebook.com/hellcrawler/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Sb0YNbuWrU>

### Licence To Hate

Death metal, Postojna

Licence To Hate were founded in 2010 and come from Postojna. The band has four members (Miha on bass, Gizo on vocals, Bajo on guitar, Tani on drums). In 2011, DIY-style in the singer's basement, they recorded four songs that together make up the demo Puppetry. In 2013 they released a second demo, Sick of All That Bleeds, and three years later a music video of the same name followed. Last September they released their first studio album, Immune to This Brutality, and they have been gigging hard across Slovenian stages ever since.
