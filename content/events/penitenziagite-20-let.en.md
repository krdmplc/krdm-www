April 2024 marks 20 years since the guys we now know as the Slovenian death metal legends Penitenziagite first got together. Their first gig only came two years later, also in Kranj.

To celebrate the anniversary and the existence of this machine, we're throwing a brutal party at the Train, where the stars of the night will of course be Penitenziagite, joined by guests from Graz, Guyođ, and Slovenian bruisers Decair.
