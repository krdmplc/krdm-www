It's been five years since an idea over a beer became official business.

So for five years now, with the help of club eMCe plac, the Šaleški študentski klub, Velehood, MOV and a few individuals without whom we wouldn't have made it, we've been working to put on a quality programme and keep alive the atmosphere and long tradition of alternative concerts in our town.

Here's to many more years of racket, screaming and headbanging!!!

### ARMAROTH

Armaroth is a five-headed entity dwelling somewhere in the obscure underground of Kranj. From its first recordings in 2008 to today, the band has established itself as one of the most brutal forces of the Slovenian metal scene. After the Burning Execution demo (2010), which only hinted at the feats to come, the band released the EP False Vision in 2013, held in high regard by fans and reviewers alike. After a few line-up changes, the singles Diamox and Deep Of Throats came out in 2016, announcing the long-awaited debut ZENITH. It was released in December of the same year and was very well received as something fresh and original on the metal scene.

Although Armaroth prefers to create in its concrete lair, it shows its true face only when it takes the stage. With natural charisma and stage presence, the band poisons and seduces the audience. It is armed with an extremely loud and precise rhythm section, fat and twisted guitar riffs and psychedelic interludes, fronted by three hellish vocals before which fear itself bows.

### DECAGE

Decage are a hard-hitting four-piece from Styria, formed in Velenje in 2015 by Aleksander Trivunovič on drums and Izi Felicijan on guitar after a local band split up. After a few line-up changes and a couple of shows, the band settled with Sebastjan Založnik on vocals and Klemen Ribič on bass and set out to metallise Slovenian soil with new, interesting, different metal. They say they play what they live: their music reflects their lives, which are different for each of them. So in their music you'll hear everything from death metal riffs to hardcore rhythms and thrash elements; they themselves call it a kind of groove/new age metal.

### MOSHEAD

A thrash metal band from Škofja Loka that started out in 2011. Soon after forming they began writing their own songs; the first one they recorded was Evil, and many more followed. In 2013 they played Vičstok alongside Tabu, Vlado Kreslin and others, and the same year they won the emerging bands contest in Kranj, which earned them a slot at the closing concert of Teden mladih with S.A.R.S. and Elvis Jackson. In 2016 they won the first season of the RTV show V svojem ritmu, and in 2017 they released their first album, Game of Knowledge. They are no strangers to big stages either, having shared them with most of the giants of Slovenian metal as well as foreign acts such as Vicious Rumors and Nervosa. Their second album, already in the works, is due in 2020.
