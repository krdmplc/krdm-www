In times of high humidity, it's easy for us to go mouldy too. That's why we've started a new series of events coloured by dark, modern dance music. Plesen ("mould") sets out to present the best names of the underground electronic scene, both established veterans and fresher blood.

At the first edition of Plesen, RSN, Douchean, S.S.S. and Selgor alongside Ofnana_pesa will get you moving on the dance floor.

Visuals by the art collective A:part.

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

### RSN (MK) (Knauf)

Macedonian artist Bojan Georgievski (aka RSN), now living in Ljubljana, is a DJ, producer and one of the founding members of the Knauf collective. He started exploring electronic music at a young age and soon found his main interest in techno. A punchy mix of drums, percussion and, above all, a dark club atmosphere can be heard in all his sets and is reflected in his own productions as well.

soundcloud.com/rsntechno

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

### Douchean (KRDM/Chagga Records)

A young producer and DJ from Kranj who explores abstract sonic forms and unknown landscapes through dirty, distorted synths and pounding rhythms. His story takes him through every possible incarnation of dark underground dance music, and his sound is marked by strong emotion and a great deal of experimentation.

Links:

https://soundcloud.com/douchean

https://douchean.bandcamp.com/releases

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

### S.S.S. (SEZAM)

The dark side of Softskinson. The alter ego of the Klub K4 resident sails through harder, punchier genres and often surprises with unconventional combinations and mixes. His sets are a blend of bliss and anger that simply can't leave a listener indifferent.

Link:

https://soundcloud.com/s-s-s-22

\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

### Selgor b2b Ofnana_pesa (both KRDM)

The two techno nightingales from Gorenjska, members of Chagga Records and Tektonika, are known for dark and hard rhythms. They first played back to back at the second edition of Tektonika and have joined forces many times since. Between them they have dozens of musical experiences across quite diverse genres, but what unites them above all is a love of underground tunes.

Links:

https://soundcloud.com/ofnana_pesa

https://soundcloud.com/selgor

https://ofnanapesa.bandcamp.com/releases
