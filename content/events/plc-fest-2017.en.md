The second edition of Plc Fest. At the time it was organised by KUD SubArt and KUD Denktimes, the society the Plc collective founded in 2016.

PLC is the name of a shelter in Kranj that is home to seven bands, an informal school of programming and many other creative activities. The collective, some 30 variously hairy heads strong, is constantly working to improve its workspaces and raise the quality of today's alternative scene in Gorenjska. In 2016 the collective founded a society, KUD Denktimes, through which Plc's two oldest bands, Didge and Armaroth, released their albums, and in 2017 you can expect new releases from some of the younger bands and plenty of concerts.

But creating, producing and at the same time upgrading the workspaces and gear doesn't come cheap, and the winter chill and the resulting need for heating only squeeze the wallets of these creative lads and lasses even harder – so they decided to put on a benefit concert, PLC Fest! The event will present almost all of Plc's musical projects, and the ticket money will go towards the winter heating bill and towards improving the rehearsal and work spaces!

### Armaroth

Death metal, Kranj

Armaroth is a five-headed entity dwelling somewhere in the obscure underground of Kranj. From their first recordings in 2008 to the present day, the band has established itself as one of the most brutal forces of the Slovenian metal scene. After the demo Burning Execution (2010), which only hinted at the real feats to come, the band released the EP False Vision in 2013, highly regarded by fans and reviewers alike. In autumn 2015 the band was reinforced by Rok Lukavečki on drums and Vid Pobegajlo on bass. A year later came the singles Diamox and Deep Of Throats, heralding the eagerly awaited debut ZENITH. It came out in December 2016 and was very well received as something fresh and original on the metal scene.

Although Armaroth prefer to create in their concrete den, they only show their true face once they take the stage. With natural charisma and stage presence, the band poisons and seduces the audience. They are armed with an extremely loud and precise rhythm section, fat and twisted guitar riffs and psychedelic touches, while at the front roars an infernal voice before which fear itself bows down.

### Nature

Progressive / technical death metal, Kranj – SPECIAL REUNION SET!!!

Nature are known to almost every metalhead in our beautiful town and its surroundings. They played their last concert at the very first PLC Fest back in 2013, so it is only right that they re-form for the event's second edition and honour us with a very special reunion set! With some fresh blood in the ranks, this sleeping beast is ready to awaken and thunder across the stages once more!

### Didge

Progressive rock, Kranj

Didge are a progressive/psychedelic rock bunch of old friends from the idyllic places north of Kranj. From humble beginnings in metal, over the years the band has developed a distinctive blend of all kinds of musical styles, unorthodox and dynamic song structures and conceptual lyrics. At the end of 2016 Didge released their debut, »Encounters In Endless Grey«, and the band is already hard at work on new material.

<https://www.facebook.com/didgeplc>

### Amalgam

Dentist metal, Kranj

A new musical formation on the local scene, made up entirely of seasoned cats from Armaroth, Raggalution, Didge and Mutism. They fling a sound rooted in progressive and mathematical styles of aggressive expression down every possible avenue of schizophrenic melodies and brawling rhythms – true Kranjscendental music.

<https://www.facebook.com/amalgamplc/>

### Sen

Progressive death / black metal, Kranj

A young and creative death metal band from Kranj, just starting its march across Slovenian stages. The band attacks with old-school genre classics and a well-honed repertoire of its own music. The lively instrumental section is commanded by the powerful and brutal voice of the petite and lovely Nina.

<https://www.facebook.com/Senplc>

### Mutism

Psychedelic / progressive, Kranj

Sold souls wander the megalomaniac spheres of sonic depiction, stopping only for the fattest and moistest slices of Zdenka's jam roll. Sometimes schnapps, too.

<https://www.facebook.com/mutismplc>

### Noč

Black metal / gangster / electronic, Gorenjska

From the Gorenjska underground, a sovereign one-man black metal act breaks through the gloomy mists, often adorning his black breviary with shavings of club-adjacent electronic currents. Add a good measure of field recordings and wide-open ambient passages, and the air quickly cools by a few degrees – until someone from the crowd gathered at this occult musical rite cries out in delirium: »Night has fallen« ("Padla je Noč").

<https://www.facebook.com/PadlaJeNoc>
