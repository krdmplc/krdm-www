KRDM in collaboration with TrainStation SubArt presents:

## DISTORŽN

At Distoržn, Siderean will play their debut album Lost on Void's Horizon in full for the first time.

**Running order**\
18:30 doors\
19:00 Ensanguinate\
20:00 Hellsword\
21:00 Siderean

### Siderean

Astral death metal, Ljubljana

Astral voyagers SIDEREAN present music from their debut Lost on Void's Horizon, recently released by the Norwegian label Edged Circle Productions. They enrich their progressive death metal grotesques with both atmosphere and dissonance, and invite you up into the heights of old-school abstraction.

<https://www.facebook.com/siderean/>\
<https://instagram.com/siderean.band>\
<https://edgedcircleproductions.bandcamp.com/album/lost-on-voids-horizon>\
<https://youtube.com/channel/UCnwss587nXAG_dxxYBvkJIA>

### Hellsword

Speed / black metal, Ljubljana

Hellsword are a trio from Ljubljana who have been on the scene since 2009. They play speed/black metal. Their music takes us back to the first wave of black metal, in other words to VENOM, BATHORY ...

<https://www.facebook.com/hellswordband>\
<https://instagram.com/hellswordlegions>\
<https://hellsword.bandcamp.com/>\
<https://youtube.com/c/HellswordIronfist>

### Ensanguinate

Death / black metal, Ljubljana

A four-piece working under the wing of Emanzipation Productions. They draw their energy from the foundations of black and death metal, and they are returning to the stage to spread the black death.

<https://www.facebook.com/ensanguinate/>\
<https://instagram.com/ensanguinate>\
<https://ensanguinate.bandcamp.com/album/entranced-by-decay>\
<https://youtube.com/channel/UCeRhDDInBmg5OCgRT1pUD9w>
