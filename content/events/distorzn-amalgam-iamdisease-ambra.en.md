KRDM, together with TrainStation SubArt, presents

## DISTORŽN

The first Distoržn of the new year will serve up a dose of anti-kvlt metal. The local Amalgam will be causing toothaches, the sludgy hardcore crew Iamdisease will be tearing down the walls, and Ambra will provide an unbearably heavy opening.

### Amalgam

Dentist metal, Kranj

A Kranj band that poached its members from every local band and put together a musical amalgam all of its own. They fling a sound rooted in progressive and mathematical styles of aggressive expression down every possible avenue of schizophrenic melodies and brawling rhythms – true Kranjscendental music.

<https://www.facebook.com/amalgamplc>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Kla4vPPS-o>

### Iamdisease

Hardcore / sludge, Primorska

Iamdisease stand on the side of dark, angry hardcore with strong metal, crust and sludge influences. Their music speaks of the dark sides of human nature and the horrors of the information society. Their message, though, is by no means surrender and despair, but hope for change and a better tomorrow for everyone.

<https://www.facebook.com/iamdisease/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYFtKeD-8OM>\
<https://iamdisease.bandcamp.com/>

### Ambra

Stoner, Ljubljana

The silence is broken, the lights are on … The foggy Ljubljana underground has spat out a hard, loud and flammable substance called Ambra. The band – Andrej Blatnik (guitar), Žiga Kroflič (guitar), Jani Premužič (vocals), Anže Vavpetič (bass) and Robi Rep (drums) – found its common denominator in a musical expression that draws inspiration from the alternative genres at the heavier end of the spectrum, without neglecting the progressive heritage it pours onto the fire in carefully measured doses.

<https://www.facebook.com/ambraband/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pTkv96FCKT4>\
<https://ambraband.bandcamp.com/releases>
