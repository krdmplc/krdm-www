### Ater Era

Black metal, Istria

The band was formed in 2006 from the ashes of the Istrian black metal band Torka. It is characterised by a primal black metal sound, extreme, long, heavy and melancholic compositions, and unusual atmospheric and psychedelic "disturbances" that express unease and revolt. They have released three albums, the latest being their most ambitious and demanding project yet. The newer material is harder to pin down by genre and differs considerably from the black metal roots of the first two albums. The songs are longer and more varied, doom and sludge are more present in the music, and an upgrade in the form of genuine psychedelia only adds to their "weight" – through which, more than ever before, they express their resistance to a psyched-out social reality.

### Morost

Complex extreme metal

"With our music we create a refuge for ourselves and those like us. A place where social maladjustment is not persecuted but valued. A place where humility in the face of the forces of nature is a form of honour."

Morost were founded in 2010 by already established Slovenian musicians, and they began appearing on stage after releasing their first album, Solace in Solitude, in 2014. The album earned positive reviews internationally as well, above all for its overall structure and the maturity of its songwriting. By creating complex metal music, Morost reach and push the boundaries of extreme musical expression, both on stage and in the studio. They have played all over Slovenia, including the main stage of Metaldays in 2019 and 2021, and have also made live debuts in Austria, Bosnia and Italy. After a few line-up changes, they went into the studio in 2020 and recorded their second album, Forged Entropy, released in March 2021 on On Parole. This album, too, was well received by critics at home and abroad.
