## DISTORŽN

This year's first Distoržn will be opened by LINTVER, GAME OVER and NIGHT CRAWLER.

### Lintver

Thrash metal, Goriška Brda

Lintver is an old-school thrash metal quartet from Goriška Brda, founded in 2004. The band already has a huge amount of live mileage behind them, as they are regular guests at Slovenian clubs and various festivals. Last year they released their third studio album, "The Worst Is Yet To Come", which they are now presenting live.

### Game Over

Thrash metal / crossover

Game Over got together back in 1988, while still teenagers. It was the first band for all of the members, who were only just learning everything together, and they started playing gigs and recording pretty quickly. They were labelled a metal/crossover band and became fairly well known. Their biggest success was playing Novi Rock '89 (headlined by Destruction), and they also played shows with Sarcasm, Epidemic Zone, Austria's Inferno ... In 1990 the tape Tilt came out. Later that same year it was all over. Later on, with a changed line-up, they released the CD Eclectric with new songs as well as old, previously unreleased recordings. After 30+ years the original four are getting back together and returning to the stage!

### Night Crawler

Thrash metal, Ljubljana / Škofja Loka

Night Crawler are a four-piece thrash metal band who are no strangers to other genres either. The band was founded in 2016 by Klemen Gabrovšek (vocals, guitar) from Ljubljana. Since then, all the members except Klemen have changed. The line-up you will see has been playing and writing mostly original music together since summer 2022. Its other members today are guitarist Ruben Škodič from Postojna, and Žiga Bizjak (bass) and Maks Kovač (drums) from Škofja Loka.
