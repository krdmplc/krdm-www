### Challenger

Heavy metal

The band stands out with catchy choruses and riffs, seasoned with a touch of progressive music and high clean vocals. Stylistically they draw on the NWOBHM school of heavy metal and on American power metal, citing influences such as Iron Maiden, Judas Priest, Satan, Omen, Riot, Cirith Ungol and others.

<https://www.facebook.com/challengerslo>\
<https://bandchallenger.bandcamp.com/album/force-of-nature>

### Street Creeps

Street rock'n'roll / heavy metal

On stage, the quartet Street Creeps radiate the genuine energy of street rock'n'roll. The music is simple and fast, and the lyrics describe life in the most out-of-the-way, darkest, dirtiest streets... Sex, drugs and street rock'n'roll! They draw ideas from various branches of heavy metal and like to flirt with other genres too. Street Creeps are fronted by the notorious heavy metal commando NJC Povše; Luka Jalšovec aka Štuhc is still holding the fort on bass from the original line-up; the ear-splitting solos and riffs are written by none other than Domen Jamnik; and on drums the versatile Kristjan Škofljanec - Kiki sets the pace.

<https://www.facebook.com/StreetCreepsBand>\
<https://www.street-creeps.com>

### Godzilla

Heavy metal

Godzilla are Katjuša, Svit, Nace and Oskar, aged between 12 and 15. They recently won the Glas mladih festival. They play classics of Slovenian metal.

<https://www.facebook.com/GodzillaBandSLO>\
<https://godzillaband.com/>
