KRDM presents the irregular traditional festival of the Kranj underground: PLC FEST 2025! Plc Fest is a festival of the musicians who rehearse in a certain air-raid shelter beneath Kranj.

This year's line-up is especially varied – we have no fewer than 7 acts! They range from bands nobody has heard of yet, through old-timers making a comeback, to regulars of the Train's stage. Come for plenty of good music and good company – and there might be a surprise or two as well!

## Playing

### Armaroth

A death metal shot to the head

<https://www.facebook.com/armaroth.band>

### Mutism

A progressive treat with a new record (soon)

Mutism will give their record its pre-premiere right here at PLC FEST 2025!

<https://www.facebook.com/mutismplc>

### GMB

Old-school metal returning for the first time in many years

GMB have got back together after 10 years exclusively for PLC FEST 2025! It would be a shame to miss them. We do hope they'll keep going afterwards and record an album.

<https://www.facebook.com/gmbkranj>

### Gründ

HC punk

Let's hope the Train doesn't go zugrunde (to ruin) when GRÜND tear it up!

<https://www.facebook.com/p/GR%C3%9CND-100063901338372/>

### DomaPaLeNismo (now Antiheroji)

Kranj punk elders gathered under a new name and with fresh momentum

DomaPaLeNismo will open PLC FEST. Even though they have the most life experience of anyone on the bill, this is their first gig ever. They play genuine comradely punk and are bursting with energy on stage. To catch them, don't be late – they start as early as 19:20!

### Golcar

The kids lighting up promising hardcore metal

Premiering at PLC FEST 2025 – Golcar! If you want to hear them, come early – they're on second.

### After: p€dri

The house Plc band

Word is that Frogi s piri will drop by PLC FEST 2025!
