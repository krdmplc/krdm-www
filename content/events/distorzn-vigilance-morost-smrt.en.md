KRDM, together with TrainStation SubArt, presents a new series of metal nights:

## DISTORŽN

The first package will blow an occult wind into Kranj: the occult heavy-metallers Vigilance from Postojna will lead us closer to the devil, the spiritually inclined death metallers Smrt from Prekmurje will warm things up, and in between Morost will bring the gloom.

### Vigilance

Speed / heavy metal, Postojna

Vigilance from Postojna play heavy/speed metal strongly reminiscent of the old sound of the N.W.O.B.H.M. wave and bands such as Angel Witch, Satan, early Maiden and so on. The band radiates an extreme old-metal aura, and a quality dose of occultism and darkness gives their themes a sharp edge. Speed metal hell!

<https://vigilanceofficial.bandcamp.com>\
<https://www.facebook.com/VigilanceAdelsberg/>

### Morost

Death / groove metal, Ljubljana

Morost are a technical band from Ljubljana composing extreme metal. Their trademark swampy groove intertwines with doom, black-metal atmosphere and unusual harmonies, and keeps returning to a death metal assault. Their debut Solace in Solitude won acclaim across the online world and was well received by those in the know. At their concerts you can also witness new material in the making, which will be released on their upcoming full-length CD.

<https://morost.bandcamp.com/>\
<https://www.facebook.com/morostband/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sY3u2h5zkwc>

### Smrt

Spiritual death metal, Turnišče

Smrt ("Death") are a four-piece spiritual death metal band from Prekmurje who recently released the demo "The Will To Power" and their first live album, "SMRT – Live at Breka". Death has no beginning. Death has no end. Death is ever present, and we all know how we will end up – with death.

<https://www.facebook.com/SMRTBand/>\
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lMRyAiFv-Mc>
