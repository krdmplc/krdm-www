Didge is a four-piece progressive rock band from Kranj. They gained a loyal following since their inception in 2012. They are driven by a passion for good and diverse music, which spans from soft, quiet parts, to Earth shattering thunder, complemented by thoughtful, carefully crafted lyrics.

Their debut LP Encounters in Endless Grey was released in 2016, followed in 2018 by the single Vesper and its music video by Filip Košnik. They also appear on all four Denktimes compilations, with Wide-Eyed Watchers, Industrial Fuckup, Vesper and Shadows We Carry By Our Side. The band is currently on hiatus.
