PESA (formerly ofnana_pesa) is a DJ, producer, bassist, event organiser… a lover of music. He started his musical journey in 2011 when he joined his first metal band. He discovered his love for electronic music a few years later and has been active in both scenes ever since.

His music is best described as eerie, dark and hard – from industrial and hard techno to cinematic soundscapes, distorted breakbeats and drum'n'bass. His productions aren't always as hard as his sets.

His music has been released on KRDM, Void Music, DE/FRAGMENT, Honest Work Recordings, Siblings Dystopian, Kibernet Kriminalität, Exaltation and Ordo Rebellium, and featured on popular techno YouTube channels such as Hate Lab, Techno Cartel and FingR86. As Nox Mastering he masters music for other artists.

He has played alongside artists such as Clara Cuve, Vera Grace, Don Woezik, Shxcxchcxsh, Lag, Rommek and Voidloss. He is a member of KRDM, founded The Void (Void Nacht) collective and label and today runs the Carnal and Ketna events. He was previously part of Chagga Records, Tektonika and Keep in Mind festival, curated the electronic programme of Trnfest for several seasons and ran the Cage stage at Drops Festival until 2022.
