Ofnana_pesa is a DJ, producer, bassist, guitarist, event organizer,.. Music lover.He started his musical career in 2011 when he joined his first metal band. Eversince he was a part of numerous musical groups and projects. Recently he startedappearing in B2B acts with Selgor more and more. As a result of him playing in  bands with hader music his DJ style would be best described as “hard, fast and eerie”.He has shared stage with artists like Shxcxchcxsh, Rommek, Voidloss, Ankur Seres,..Besides making music he is an organizer in Tektonika collective, a member of Chagga recs and KRDM. He released his first single “Immortale” on KRDM in 2019 that can be found on Hate Lab’s Youtube channel.
