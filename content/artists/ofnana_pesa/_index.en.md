Ofnana_pesa is an industrial/dark techno DJ, producer, and event organizer from Slovenia. His music combines hypnotic, electronic soundscapes with aggressive, dark beats and elements of industrial music. As an organizer of darker, faster style of techno music he has founded several collectives and events, including Void Nacht, Tektonika, and Ketna, providing many events for experimental and industrial techno artists to showcase their work.

Through his music, ofnana_pesa creates immersive soundscapes that transport listeners to otherworldly realms, while his DJ sets often take on a more raw and intense energy. His hypnotic and driving beats have garnered him a following among fans of darker music.
