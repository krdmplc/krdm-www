GRÜND were a hardcore punk band from Kranj who fired off short, fast and angry songs with a dose of crust and grind from the Plc bunker between 2020 and 2025. In their own words: "Gründ je polom, raj vilco u štrom."

Their first gig was in summer 2021 at Trainstation Subart. After that they kept the pit spinning at Metelkova and in Maribor, Škofja Loka and Zagreb, and even made it onto Bosnian TV. They left behind the split Poslednje rogoviljenje with Opran Možgan (2021), a benefit for the people evicted from Rog, and the album Iz pizde v pizdo (2022), recorded at Studio Subart. At Plc Fest 2025 they turned the hall into a gym one last time.
