Nesit were a young band from the Plc bunker, in their own words "a bunch of young music enthusiasts trying to combine all kinds of musical styles into interesting combinations and fairly listenable music".

They played their first concert at Plc Fest on 5 May 2018 at Trainstation Subart, and that summer they appeared on the Denktimes Vol. 3 compilation with the track Excess. After that, only silence and one concert video remain.
