Kranj's Raggalution have been stirring their reggae stew, seasoned with dub, funk and a pinch of psychedelia, since 2011. They used to rehearse at Plc – members also played in Amalgam – and today they go their own way.

Touring took their 2013 debut Mask of Liberty from Greece to the Netherlands, followed by the albums Gatekeeper (2017) and Improterrestrial. They still regularly fill Kranj's stages, including Subart festival and their own Raggastation nights.
