Antiheroji (The Antiheroes) play the punk today's world needs again – and the punk Gorenjska heard before it was fashionable. The band was started in 1979 in Naklo by Samo and Uroš, who caught their first punk songs on Radio Luxembourg and Radio Ljubljana. They put their 14-year-old neighbour Sergej behind the drums, Sergej brought in Ivan on bass, and a classic punk band was born: two punks and two musicians.

They played their first gig on 22 December 1979 in the Naklo community hall and, as far as they know, were the fourth punk band in Slovenia and the first outside Ljubljana. In spring 1980 Uroš was called up for military service and the band fell apart, but songs like Mladinski funkcionar (Youth Functionary) and Suženj (Slave) outlived every regime – in people's heads and in Naklo's dive bars. In 2019 they celebrated their 40th anniversary in the same hall and re-recorded Mladinski funkcionar at Trainstation in Kranj, and in March 2025 the punk elders returned to the stage at PLC Fest – first under the name DomaPaLeNismo, and now once again as Antiheroji, because the functionaries are still among us.
