"Created by Darkness, and by Darkness reclaimed, so that we can unleash It once more."

A black metal band from Preddvor and Kranj, one of the first to rehearse at Plc. They started in 2006 with heavy metal and moved through melodic and symphonic black/death metal with synths to pure black metal about ravens, slavery and dreams. They released the Forever Enslaved EP in 2009 and said goodbye in 2011 with the live recording Live Enslaved. Their drummer later played in Armaroth.
