Selgor is a dj, producer and multimedia artist. His musical journey started in early 2010 with electronic trio Milk Drinkers. Four years later he decided to leave the band and continue on his own with faster, harder and more surreal music style. He likes to combine industrial dark techno beats with psychedelic and acid inserts. In his collaboration with ofnana_pesa, he gets even more dark, brutal and heavy as they are surfing together on more intense soundwaves. In November 2019 he became a part of fresh and creative KRDM label.
