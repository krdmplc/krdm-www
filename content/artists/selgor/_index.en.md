Selgor is a DJ, producer and multimedia artist. His musical journey started in early 2010 with the electronic trio Milk Drinkers. Four years later he decided to leave the band and continue on his own with a faster, harder and more surreal style. He likes to combine industrial dark techno beats with psychedelic and acid inserts, but can also surprise with lighter, hypnotic moments or more complex rhythms. In his collaboration with ofnana_pesa he gets even darker, more brutal and heavier, as they surf together on more intense soundwaves. In November 2019 he became part of the fresh and creative KRDM collective and label.

He co-founded and is a resident of the Ljubljana label DE/FRAGMENT, appearing on each of its annual compilations (Weapons of Mass Destruction, Crush, Calling My Existence, Computer, Habitat), and was part of The Void, Tektonika and a:part collectives. He directed and animated the music videos for Crush and Calling My Existence himself. As a filmmaker he won the Vesna award for best experimental film at the Festival of Slovenian Film 2026 for Deveti vrh.
