Golcar are Kranj hardcore – Kransterdam Hardcore, to be precise. The younger generation of the local scene, they go all in on beatdown: heavy riffs, groove you feel in your gut and breakdowns as slow as a Monday morning. On stage since 2025, the band has earned a reputation in just over a year as a crew that says the most when it plays live, as audiences at PLC Fest and the local-scene nights at SubArt festival in Trainstation found out.
