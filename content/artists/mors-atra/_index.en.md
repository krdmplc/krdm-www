Mors Atra blend brutal technical death metal, deathcore, symphonic and black metal into a fast, melodic and orchestrated beast. Shredding guitars, earth-shattering grooves and ferocious vocals, enough to shake the walls.

After early shows at Metelkova and Orto bar they tore up Plc Fest 2023, and in December 2024 they released their debut album Eternal Rest in cooperation with KRDM, mastered by Vid Pobegajlo (Nox Mastering). Line-up: Vid Fekonja (vocals), Matej Oblak (lead guitar, bass), Rok Fekonja (rhythm guitar) and Simon Intihar (drums).
