From the Gorenjska underground, a sovereign one-man black metal project breaks through the gloomy mist, often decorating his black breviary with shavings of off-club electronic currents. Add a good dose of field recordings and wide ambient passages, and the air quickly cools by a few degrees, until someone at the occult musical rite screams in delirium: "Night has fallen."

The one-man project of Nature's singer released twelve demos between 2015 and 2018, followed in 2021 by the singles Merging Streams and Don't Stare Into The Portal. Noč played Plc Fest 2017, and the Denktimes compilations feature The Struggle Itself Towards The Heights and The Curse of the Normies.
