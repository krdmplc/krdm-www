Krive spohe are cartoons grown out of Plc mischief: short animated sketches drawn and edited in Blender, "more or less based on real events". Behind them is Aleš Jelovčan, often with animator Rok Vilar.

The series started in 2020 with Štop and hit home with Kje je frogi s piri?!, made at a Blender workshop at Trainstation Subart, which the whole hall sings along to at Plc Fest. Krive spohe also make videos for our bands: GMB's Nebelscheinwerfer and Which Animal Would You Like To Be?, Oti's What If, Armaroth's Lokalna scena live video and a Distoržn promo.
