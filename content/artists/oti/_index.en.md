Oti is a lost boy who found his home in music – the indie rock project of singer-songwriter, guitarist and academic composer Otmar Taber. He calls his genre maximalist popular music: in the studio that means strings, harpsichord, theremin and songs that refuse to stop at three minutes, while on stage the songs slip into something more intimate – as a trio with Luka Dobnikar on bass and Aleš Kozelj on drums, or completely solo.

After the singles Barve (2022), So You Say (2023) and What If (2026), Oti self-released the Samospev EP in November 2026: four songs recorded with a string trio and producer Grega Kalinski, and the fullest expression of his vision so far. The record was launched at Gala Hala in Ljubljana on 14 November 2026, and on 27 November Oti joins the crew at PLC Fest in Trainstation, Kranj.
