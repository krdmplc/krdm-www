Oti is a lost boy who found his home in music. He is the indie rock project of singer-songwriter, guitarist and academic composer Otmar Taber. He calls his genre maximalist popular music – in the studio that means rich arrangements and songs that refuse to stop at three minutes, while live he performs in a more intimate guise, either as a trio or solo.

After the singles Barve (2022), So You Say (2023) and What If (2026), he self-released the Samospev EP in November 2026, the fullest expression of his artistic vision so far. The short-form debut was launched at Gala Hala in Ljubljana on 14 November 2026, and on 27 November he plays PLC Fest at Trainstation in Kranj.
