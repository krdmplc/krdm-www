Ensanguinate play "the METAL OF DEATH": they draw on the foundations of black and death metal and on bands such as Morbid Angel, Grotesque, Dissection and The Devil's Blood.

Around Plc Fest 2023 they rehearsed at Plc – "the Kranj bunker suits them perfectly", they said when Paranoid filmed a rehearsal session there in 2022 – and they played Distoržn in 2021 and Plc Fest 2023. After the demo Entranced by Decay (2020) they released the albums Eldritch Anatomy (2022) and Death Saturnalia (With Temples Below) (2026, Soulseller Records).
