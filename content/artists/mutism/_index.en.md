Four cowboys sit around a storage heater and eat chocolate pudding. Who will be left without a spoon after dinner? There is enough meat for everyone. Trash lies everywhere. In the distance, dancing cacti. Are you drinking enough water?

Mutism ride the cosmic plains of art and prog rock. Their music is marked by complex structures and an ever-present melancholic aura; unpredictable instrumental turns meet emotional lyrics and sky-reaching choruses, delivered to your moist ears by a sensual, expressive female voice. A prog rock gourmet buffet.

After a decade in Kranj's Plc bunker and plenty of line-up changes, they released their debut album Bitter Blossom in April 2026. In 2025 they supported Sweden's Soen at Subart festival, among other shows.
