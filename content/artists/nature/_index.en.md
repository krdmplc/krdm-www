Nature are a long lasting and well known name in Slovenian death metal. With some fresh blood in the ranks, they sound better than ever. They rose from the ashes of Sinbolic, and their music leans towards the progressive spectrum and is influenced by many other genres.

Their studio recordings can be found on the Denktimes compilations – Hospitalized, Earth and Purge the Waters. After a long break, the "death metal dads" return to the stage at PLC Fest 2026.
