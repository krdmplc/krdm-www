A technical death metal entity from Gorenjska and once the youngest band at Plc. Demanding death metal with clear growls, dreamy guitar passages and trading solos that leave no music connoisseur indifferent.

Active from 2012 to 2014, they opened the very first Plc Fest in 2013, and in April 2014, with a new drummer, renamed themselves The Pattern and released a music video for Discordia. Members later went on to bands such as Mutism and Oti.
