A young, creative progressive death metal band from Kranj who in 2016 and 2017 attacked with old-school genre classics and a well-honed set of their own songs, led by strong, brutal vocals.

They played Plc Fest 2017, and their song Animal is on the Denktimes compilations Vol. 1 and 2. Two of the members now play in Mutism.
