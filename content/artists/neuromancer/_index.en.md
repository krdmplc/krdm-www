Neuromancer is a synthwave duo from Kranj that mixes modern compositions with the sound of the 80s. They emerged in 2018 with their first single Hyperdrive, followed by Dead Streets, Cybercop, Apollo, Inhuman, Mainframe Terror and Cloud City.

Their debut album Hardwired came out in September 2023 on the US label NewRetroWave (also on vinyl, cassette and MiniDisc) and topped Bandcamp's synthwave album sales chart. In 2024 they contributed Running Is Futile to the soundtrack of the documentary Aliens Expanded. They have supported Igorrr and Carpenter Brut at Kino Šiška and played MENT, Tolminator and Subart festivals as well as Ljubljana's New Year's Eve celebration.

On their live performances expect a journey across the entire spectrum of emotions, from melancholic pianos and badass guitars to the heavy thunder of electronic sounds, captured between future and past.
