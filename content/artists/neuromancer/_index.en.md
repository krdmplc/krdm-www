Neuromancer is a synthwave duo from Kranj that mixes modern compositions with the sound of the 80s. They emerged in 2018, when they released their first single Hyperdrive. Currently they are working on their debut LP Inhuman, which will be out soon. On their live performances expect a journey across the entire spectrum of emotions, captured between future and past.
