Neuromancer is a synthwave duo that mixes modern compositions with the sound of the 80s. They emerged in 2018, whn they released their first single Hypoerdrive. Currently they are working on their debut LP Inhuman, which will be out soon. On their live performances expect a yourney on entire spectrum of emotions, captured between future and past.
