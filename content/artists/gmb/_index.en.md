GMB are death metallers from Kranj who have been dishing out fast, groovy and greasy death metal with a crossover edge from the Plc bunker since 2011. They are among the heavier bands in Slovenia, and their motto, "Happy, fat, strong and bouncy", shows in both the music and the members themselves.

After their first demo Gore Obsessed Violent Exhibition (2013) and a decade-long break, they returned in 2025 with the singles Nebelscheinwerfer and Which Animal Would You Like To Be?, followed in June 2026 by their self-titled debut album, launched at the Release the Dead show in Medvode.
