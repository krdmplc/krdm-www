A producer, DJ and sound engineer from Kranj who travels through abstract sonic landscapes and delivers dirty, distorted synthesizers and pounding rhythms. His story leads him through every possible incarnation of dark underground dance music – at the crossroads of techno, electro, EBM and bass music – and strong emotion and experimentation are the signature of his sound.

After his debut A Lumberjack's Smile (2019) he co-founded the Ljubljana label and event series DE/FRAGMENT, where he released the EPs It Was Obviously Colonel Mustard With the Candlestick in the Library (2022), Aphrodisiac and Body Worship (2024). His album The Relentless Struggle Of Gravity came out on the UK label Singularity Recordings in 2022. He has played MENT and Drops festivals, among others, and is now also part of the drum'n'bass collective Frag::ments.
