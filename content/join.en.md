KRDM isn't an agency or a club: it's a group of friends sharing a concrete bunker in Planina, Kranj – Plc. Since 2009, bands, DJs and other artists from Kranj have rehearsed, recorded and hung out there. The association looks after the space, puts on the Distoržn concerts and Plc Fest, and releases its members' music.

## Who Plc is for

- **Bands and musicians** looking for a rehearsal space in Kranj who are happy to share time slots, gear and responsibility for the space.
- **Independent artists** – DJs, producers, illustrators, animators and others who need a corner to create in.
- **Organisers and volunteers** who'd like to help at concerts: on the door, behind the bar, with sound, posters, photos or social media.

## What being a member means

We run Plc ourselves. Members share the care of the space, the gear and the running costs, split the rehearsal schedule and help out at the association's events. In return you get a place where you can be loud, people to make things with, and a stage at Plc Fest.

## How to join

The space is small and slots are limited, so first we get to know each other. Tell us who you are and what you make, and we'll meet up at Plc for a chat.
